Method. Week 1: shared recipes create weighted ingredient links. Week 6: cosine similarity measures association. Each ingredient appears as one dot inside a country or approximate region linked to its strongest cuisine association; positions do not represent origins. Dot size follows its recipe count on a compressed scale; color marks its strongest relative cuisine association. Click a dot to draw its 30 most frequent co-occurring ingredient pairs, or choose all matching pairs. Thicker, brighter links represent pairs seen together more often; each link takes the connected ingredient's cuisine color. The minimum recipes per pair filter applies to these lines. Ingredients without a clear geographic cuisine match use the labelled Antarctica holding area. Area lists rank ingredients by their cuisine recipe rate divided by their overall recipe rate, with at least five cuisine recipes. Map boundaries: Natural Earth (public domain).
